Frequently Asked Questions

General Questions

How can I contact the organizers?
You can reach out to us via email at ewrl-2026@inria.fr
My paper is accepted but I do not have an url for the paper yet. What do I do?
Please say on the 'Publication Link' in openreview 'I will send the confirmation by email' and then forward us (at ewrl-2026@inria.fr) the email in which you got the acceptation for your paper.
I have a position paper accepted at some famous conference, can I submit it at EWRL 2026?
We do not accept position paper, only research papers are considered at EWRL 2026.

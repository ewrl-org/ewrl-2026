Description

The European Workshop on Reinforcement Learning (EWRL) is the gathering hub of the Reinforcement Learning research community in Europe and one of the main world-class events in RL. We plan to make the 2026 edition an exciting event for researchers worldwide, both for the presentation of top quality papers, but also as a forum for ample discussion of open problems and future research directions.

Past editions of EWRL can be found here.

Topics

Reinforcement learning is an active field of research which deals with the problem of sequential decision making in unknown (and often) stochastic and/or partially observable environments. Recently there has been a wealth of impressive empirical results, including those coupling Deep Learning function approximators with Reinforcement Learning algorithms, as well as significant theoretical advances. Both types of advances are of major importance and we aim to create a forum to discuss such interesting results, their applications, consequences, and research perspectives.

We encourage submissions in both theoretical and practical aspects of Reinforcement Learning.

Keynote Speakers

Christian Duriez
DEFROST, Inria, Univ Lille, CNRS, Ecole Centrale de Lille, Compliance Robotics, France
Christian Duriez is a research director at Inria Lille, France, where he leads the DEFROST team. He earned his Ph.D. in Robotics from the CEA and Université d’Évry in 2004, followed by a postdoctoral position at CIMIT SimGroup in Boston, USA. His research focuses on interactive simulation of deformable objects, haptic rendering, and surgical simulation, with a strong emphasis on soft robotics modeling and control, fast finite element methods, and contact response simulation. A key contributor to the open-source SOFA framework, he co-founded InSimo (2013) and Compliance Robotics (2024), where he advances the use of soft robots for educational and industrial applications, particularly in the food industry.
Nathan Grinsztajn
Mistral.
Nathan Grinsztajn works on agents at Mistral AI. Previously, he was a Member of Technical Staff at Cohere, where he worked on RL for LLMs and reasoning, and before that a research scientist at InstaDeep, where he focused on transformers for combinatorial optimization and discrete problems.
Anders Jonsson
Universitat Pompeu Fabra.
Anders Jonsson is a full professor in the Department of Engineering, where he heads the Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning research group. His research interests involve sequential decision problems in which one or several agents have to make repeated decisions about what to do, mainly in the form of automated planning and reinforcement learning.
Sao Mai Nguyen
Ensta-Paris.
Sao Mai Nguyen is an associate professor at Ensta-Paris. She is interested in robotics, artificial intelligence and cognitive science. She tries to bridge these 3 aspects in her work, and started working in cognitive developmental robotics. She has a special interest in linking research of machine learning, robotic embodiment, child development psychology and neuroscience.
Alexandre Proutiere
KTH, Royal Institute of Technology.
Alexandre Proutiere is a professor at the Decision and Control Systems division in the EECS school at KTH, Royal Institute of Technology. His research interests are Machine Learning and AI, reinforcement learning, unsupervised learning, statistics, stochastic processes, control; applications to communication and social networks, cybersecurity.
Giorgia Ramponi
University of Zurich.
Giorgia is an Assistant Professor with Tenure Track at the Faculty of Business, Economics, and Informatics at the University of Zurich. She is also an affiliated professor at the ETH AI Center and an affiliated professor at the Data Science and AI, Computer Science, and Engineering department at Chalmers University of Technology.
Pierre Schegg
CAMIN, Inria, LIRMM, Univ Montpellier, Montpellier, France
Pierre Schegg is a Post-Doc at Inria, University. He works on controlling soft robots in particular for use in medical applications such as Cardiology and Bariatric Surgery.
Aviv Tamar
Technion ECE.
Aviv Tamar is an associate professor at Technion ECE, where he heads the Robot Learning Lab. His research focuses on AI and machine learning, with an emphasis on robotics applications. His long term goal is to bring robots into human-centered domains such as homes and hospitals.
